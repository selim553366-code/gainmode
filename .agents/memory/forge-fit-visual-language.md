name: Forge Fit visual language
description: The product-level visual direction for continuing Forge Fit screen redesigns.
---

Forge Fit should use a light, spacious blue-white product interface: pale atmospheric backgrounds, vivid blue gradient accents, rounded elevated cards, soft shadows, and restrained translucent glass controls for secondary or contextual actions. Primary actions may stay solid blue when stronger hierarchy or contrast is needed. Onboarding's highlighted opening text and Continue button should be plainly blue, without neon, gradient, glass, or shine effects; preserve strong contrast for the button label.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose a clean premium fitness-product direction inspired by generous whitespace, translucent buttons, thin borders, soft glow, and rounded media-led cards, then specifically corrected onboarding to use simple blue text and a flat blue Continue button. A theme's general primary color may be white in dark mode, making a white-on-white CTA invisible.

**How to apply:** Keep the shared theme tokens as the source of truth, use semantic foreground/card colors instead of black/white shortcuts in dark mode, preserve strong text contrast and mobile hit targets, and carry the same visual language across onboarding, premium, home, nutrition, coach, plan, and progress without changing i18n or product behavior.
