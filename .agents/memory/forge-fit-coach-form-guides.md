---
name: Forge Fit coach form guides
description: Visual and interaction decision for exercise form guidance across the workout library.
---

Exercise form guidance uses the app’s friendly translucent blue coach as the recurring demonstrator. Each guide shows movement stages above a localized written cue, with movement variants grouped into reusable form families.

**Why:** Reusing one recognizable coach and a small set of biomechanically related form families keeps the library visually consistent and avoids a separate, heavy asset for every equipment variant.

**How to apply:** Keep the guide visual square, avoid baked-in text, map every exercise key to a form family, and keep all written guidance localized across the five supported languages.
